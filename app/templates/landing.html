Everything your network team needs

From a single address to an entire datacenter, manage it all with confidence.

IP Address Inventory

Track IPv4/IPv6 addresses with status, VLAN, MAC, hostname, allocation type and full ownership metadata.

Subnet Management

Organize networks by CIDR, role and location with broadcast addresses and VLAN association.

Device Tracking

Maintain a hardware inventory with vendor, model, OS, firmware, serial numbers and rack locations.

Role-Based Access

Administrators manage data and users; view-only members get safe, read-only access. Enforced server-side.

Audit History

Every IP change is recorded with before/after values, timestamps and the acting user.

REST API

Automate workflows through a documented, token-authenticated API for IPs, networks, devices and users.
