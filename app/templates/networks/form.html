Helpful tips
  • Use full CIDR notation, e.g. 192.168.1.0/24.
  • The address family follows the CIDR, IPv4 or IPv6.
  • Gateway, DHCP and VLAN ranges must sit inside the CIDR.
  • Overlapping CIDRs are rejected, so keep aggregations on the parent network.
  • A parent network must contain this subnet, which keeps the hierarchy honest.
